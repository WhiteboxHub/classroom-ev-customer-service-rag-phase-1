# High-Voltage Service Procedures — EV-3000 / EV-5000
**Document:** SM-HV-001 | **Version:** 5.0 | **Classification:** TECHNICIAN ONLY
**WARNING: HIGH-VOLTAGE PROCEDURES — CERTIFIED EV TECHNICIANS ONLY**

## Safety Requirements
**MANDATORY PPE for all HV procedures:**
- Class 0 insulated rubber gloves (1000V rated), tested within 6 months
- Safety glasses or face shield
- HV-rated insulated hand tools (1000V rated, red handle with VDE symbol)
- High-visibility, non-conductive safety footwear
- Cotton or natural fiber clothing only — no synthetic materials

**MANDATORY LOCK OUT / TAG OUT (LOTO):**
- Apply LOTO device to manual service disconnect before any HV work
- Verify isolation with CAT III or CAT IV multimeter rated for > 1000V DC

## Manual Service Disconnect (MSD) Procedure

### Location
- **EV-3000:** Rear trunk, lift trunk floor mat, remove plastic cover — orange MSD handle visible
- **EV-5000:** Under rear seat, remove seat cushion using T40 Torx — orange MSD handle under foam pad

### Procedure
**Step 1:** Power off vehicle completely — remove key fob from vehicle vicinity (minimum 10 feet)
**Step 2:** Open hood / trunk as per vehicle model (see location above)
**Step 3:** Wait minimum **5 minutes** for HV capacitors to discharge
**Step 4:** Put on Class 0 insulated gloves
**Step 5:** Locate orange MSD handle — DO NOT touch adjacent orange HV cables
**Step 6:** Pull MSD handle UPWARD firmly with both hands until it disengages (approximately 30 lbs pull force)
**Step 7:** Verify isolation: measure voltage between HV+ (red) and HV- (black) terminals using CAT III multimeter — MUST read < 60V DC before proceeding
**Step 8:** Apply LOTO device to MSD port
**Step 9:** Tag vehicle: "HV ISOLATED — DO NOT ENERGIZE"

### MSD Re-engagement
**Step 1:** Remove LOTO device
**Step 2:** Align MSD connector and push DOWN firmly until you hear and feel a click
**Step 3:** Verify all tools and personnel are clear of HV areas
**Step 4:** Power on vehicle and check for HV-related DTCs

## HV Battery Inspection (Visual Only)
**WARNING: Do NOT open battery pack enclosure in field — workshop environment only.**

Allowed field inspection:
- Inspect battery pack mounting bolts for looseness
- Check HV cables for visible damage, chafing, or heat discoloration
- Check HV cable connectors for secure locking (orange lever fully seated)
- Inspect battery coolant hose connections for leaks (look for orange coolant residue)
